Preparing for the Move

Florida Moving Tips

A successful move involves more than packing boxes. Planning utilities, documents, transportation, insurance, and the first few days in your new community can make the transition much easier.

01

Before the Move

Create a moving timeline, compare moving companies, organize important documents, and confirm the date that utilities will begin at your new home.

02

Update Important Information

Make a list of organizations that need your new address. Include financial accounts, insurance providers, subscriptions, employers, schools, and other important contacts.

03

Prepare for Arrival

Keep medications, chargers, clothing, toiletries, documents, basic tools, and other necessities separate from items that will be packed away.

04

Learn Your New Area

Locate nearby grocery stores, medical facilities, gas stations, schools, emergency services, and major routes before you need them.

Quick Checklist

Before Moving Day

  • Confirm moving and arrival dates.
  • Arrange electricity, water, internet, and other utilities.
  • Keep important documents accessible.
  • Prepare an essentials bag for the first few days.
  • Review your route and expected tolls.
  • Check the weather before traveling.
  • Keep emergency contacts readily available.
