Choosing a City in Florida

Florida is a large and diverse state, and choosing where to live involves much more than deciding how close you want to be to the beach. Climate, housing, employment, transportation, recreation, and community size can vary considerably from one region to another.

Select one of the regions below to explore some of the communities, scenery, and characteristics that make each part of Florida different.

Central Florida

Orlando • Kissimmee • Lakeland

Central Florida combines growing metropolitan areas with suburban communities, lakes, natural springs, entertainment, and a convenient location near the center of the state. The region offers employment in tourism, health care, education, technology, construction, and many other industries.

Comparing Florida Regions

No single region is the right choice for everyone. Your decision may depend on employment, housing, transportation, climate, recreation, and the type of community you prefer. This table provides a starting point for comparing the four areas featured above.

Region Example Communities General Character Consider If You Want
Central Florida Orlando, Kissimmee, Lakeland Inland metropolitan and suburban communities A central location with varied employment and recreation
Tampa Bay & Gulf Coast Tampa, St. Petersburg, Clearwater Metropolitan communities with Gulf Coast access City amenities combined with coastal recreation
South Florida Miami, Fort Lauderdale, West Palm Beach Dense, coastal, international metropolitan areas Urban living, nightlife, beaches, and year-round warmth
North Florida Jacksonville, Tallahassee, Gainesville Large cities mixed with smaller communities, rivers, and forests A less tropical environment and access to major universities and government centers

Look Beyond the Map

Before choosing a community, consider more than its location. Research commute times, employment opportunities, housing, insurance, schools, health care, transportation, recreation, and the overall pace of life. If possible, spend time in the area before making a final decision so you can experience what everyday life there is actually like.
